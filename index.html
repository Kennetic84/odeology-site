About

Odeology is a volunteer-run collective putting on techno nights in Odense.

We book dark, hypnotic techno from Denmark and abroad, and light is a big part of every night, with lasers, LED walls and visuals built for the room.

Every night is built by people who love the music: crew, artists and volunteers.
